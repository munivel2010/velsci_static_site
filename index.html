Velsci Globals strictly complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679.

All body measurements and optional reference uploads submitted via Velsci Fit are used solely for pattern calculation and digital rendering. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

To request data inspection or complete deletion, contact info@velsci.com.
