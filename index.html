Simple & Digital

How It Works

Three seamless photo-reference steps connecting European users to Indian artisans.

01

Photo-Reference Scan

Upload two non-intrusive full-length reference photos using standard clothing as a scale benchmark. No complex 3D body scanners required.

Photos are processed securely and deleted post-cutting.
02

Artisan Crafting

Our system generates digital pattern vectors and transmits them directly to certified master tailors in India, providing stable, year-round income.

Fair trade wages paid directly to master tailors.
03

Global Delivery & Care

Your tailored outfit is delivered to your doorstep in Europe, complete with a circular care passport and option for future care preservation.

Shipped with carbon-neutral logistics tracking.
