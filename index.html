Our Mission & Heart

Born from a deeply personal journey of resilience and gratitude, Velsci Globals was created to uplift traditional artisan communities in India. By replacing mass fast-fashion overproduction with on-demand precision, we guarantee fair living wages for local craftspeople while offering high-quality, circular fashion solutions to global consumers.
