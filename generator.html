No Measuring Tape? Alternative Methods

1. 🧵 Thread / String + Standard Ruler

Wrap any thread around body points, mark with a pen, and lay flat against a standard 15cm / 30cm ruler.

2. 📄 Standard A4 Paper Dimensions

A4 paper is exactly 29.7 cm (11.7 in) long and 21.0 cm (8.3 in) wide. Fold in half for quick scaling.

3. 📱 Smartphone AR Camera Apps

Use built-in iOS "Measure" app or Android "AR Ruler" to scan body points directly.
