No Measuring Tape? Alternative Methods

1. 🧵 Thread / String + Standard Ruler

Wrap any piece of thread or ribbon around your body, mark the point with a pen, and lay it flat against a standard 15cm/30cm school ruler.

2. 📄 Standard A4 Paper Dimensions

Standard A4 paper is exactly 29.7 cm (11.7 in) long and 21.0 cm (8.3 in) wide. Fold in half to quickly estimate measurements.

3. 📱 Smartphone AR Camera Apps

Use built-in iOS "Measure" app or Android "AR Ruler" to point your camera at clothing or body landmarks for quick sizing.
