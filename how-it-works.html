How Velsci Globals Works

Connecting European technology with South Asian master craftsmanship through a seamless 4-step digital ecosystem.

01

Easy Digital Input

Upload two simple photo references using standard well-fitting clothing as a benchmark, or share preferred custom measurements through our secure digital portal—all from the comfort of your home.

100% GDPR Compliant & Secure Photo Handling
02

Digital Pattern Vectoring

Our specialized algorithmic engine converts sizing data into high-precision digital vector patterns. This removes traditional fitting error margins and optimizes fabric usage to guarantee zero material waste.

Precision Automated Vector Output
03

Master Artisan Crafting

Verified master tailors in India directly access digital patterns to tailor your custom apparel. By cutting out middle distributors, we ensure artisans earn sustainable, fair-trade wages for every piece made.

Direct Support for Heritage Artisans
04

Doorstep Delivery & Circular Care

Your tailored garment arrives at your doorstep in Europe along with a Digital Garment Passport. This passport unlocks options for fabric preservation, eco-cleaning, or circular buyback swaps.

Integrated Circular Garment Passport
